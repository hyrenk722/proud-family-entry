Entry for the make.fun competition "Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family
Scene": one original short family scene, a single line of at most 280
characters. `main.py` writes that scene to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), validating the gate first: UTF-8 text,
1–280 characters, not only whitespace, one or two lines — then reading the
file back to verify the written bytes match.

> Every Christmas my dad hangs the same crooked star on the tree, then stands back and says, "There. Now it looks like us."

Why this scene: the whole moment turns on one crooked star. Dad hangs it
the same way every year, steps back, and claims the imperfection as the
family resemblance — warm, a little funny, and true in the way only a
household's own joke can be. One line, no wasted word, and the last four
words carry all the pride.
